How to play

  1. 01

    Take turns dropping your colored disc into one of the 7 columns.

  2. 02

    Your disc falls to the lowest empty spot in that column.

  3. 03

    Connect 4 in a row — across, up and down, or diagonally — to win.

  4. 04

    If the board fills before anyone connects four, it’s a draw.
